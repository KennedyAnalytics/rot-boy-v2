# The Corporate Defector — Character Bible v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED / CANONICAL  
**Canonical visual reference:** `Hooded Corporate Defector With Tablet.png`  
**Effective date:** 2026-10-04  
**Purpose:** Preserve the exact identity of the approved Corporate Defector across poses, props, transparent assets, and motion-graphics compositions.

## 1. Source-of-truth rule

The approved canonical image is the primary visual source of truth. This Character Bible translates that image into repeatable production rules; it does not authorize aesthetic redesign.

When references conflict, use this order:

1. The approved canonical image.
2. The **LOCKED** rules in this Character Bible.
3. Pose-, scene-, or prop-specific instructions.

Future generations must use the canonical image as an identity reference whenever the production method allows it. Text prompting alone is not sufficient for identity-critical assets.

## 2. Status definitions

- **LOCKED:** Defines the character. Must remain stable. Any intentional change requires explicit approval and a new Character Bible version.
- **FLEXIBLE:** May change naturally with pose, camera angle, expression, lighting, or scene needs while remaining recognizably the same character.
- **OPTIONAL/PROP:** Not inherent to the character. Include only when required by the shot or script.

## 3. Core identity summary — LOCKED

The Corporate Defector is a dark-skinned adult Black man rendered in a clean, grounded anime illustration style. He has a lean, long-legged build; short, clearly visible 360 waves with a precise hairline; strong almond-shaped eyes and angular eyebrows; a fitted charcoal lower-face mask; a separate folded charcoal neck gaiter; and a raised charcoal hood that creates a distinctive pointed arch around his head. His wardrobe combines corporate and technical streetwear: an open charcoal hoodie over a pale blue pinstripe button-down shirt, a plain navy corporate lanyard with a blank badge, dark symmetrical cargo pants, and chunky gray technical sneakers.

The design must continue to read as **corporate employee turned covert AI operator**, not as a superhero, soldier, hacker stereotype, ninja, or armored cyberpunk character.

## 4. Identification specifications

### 4.1 Hairstyle and hairline — LOCKED

- Short African-American 360 waves.
- Hair is visibly longer than a skin-close buzz cut; the wave ridges and natural hair texture must remain readable.
- Clean, sharp, squared front hairline with neat temple edges.
- Low, close silhouette that fits naturally beneath the raised hood.
- Hair color is natural near-black.
- No braids, twists, cornrows, dreadlocks, hanging curls, loose fringe, high-top shape, fade design, or bald appearance.
- The amount of visible wave pattern may reduce with perspective or hood occlusion, but the hairstyle must not change.

### 4.2 Face, eyes, and visible skin — LOCKED

- Adult Black male facial identity.
- Warm medium-deep brown skin with restrained cel-shaded highlights and shadows.
- Strong almond-shaped eyes with dark irises, clean white sclera, dark upper lash line, and a focused forward intensity.
- Thick, dark, sharply shaped eyebrows that angle slightly downward toward the center.
- Moderate brow-to-eye spacing and a firm, controlled expression.
- Ears are proportionate and may be visible inside the hood opening.
- Facial structure, eye spacing, eye shape, eyebrow thickness, forehead height, skin tone, and hairline must not drift.
- Do not lighten the skin, change ethnicity, enlarge the eyes into a youthful anime style, or make the face photorealistic.

### 4.3 Expression and gaze — FLEXIBLE

- Eye direction may follow the subject of the scene, point toward on-screen information, or address the viewer.
- Brows and eyelids may communicate focus, concern, skepticism, realization, or restrained confidence.
- Emotional variation should remain controlled and adult. Avoid exaggerated comedy faces, glowing eyes, extreme rage, or childlike expressions.

### 4.4 Face mask — LOCKED

- Matte charcoal fitted mask covering nose, mouth, cheeks, and chin.
- Clean, slightly angular upper edge that sits across the bridge of the nose and slopes toward the cheeks.
- Minimal faceted shading around the nose; no printed design, vents, logo, teeth, skull motif, or visible mouth.
- The mask is a separate item from the neck gaiter.
- The mask remains worn in standard character appearances unless its removal is explicitly requested.

### 4.5 Neck gaiter — LOCKED

- Separate dark charcoal fabric wrap beneath the mask.
- Several broad, soft horizontal folds create a compact layered cowl around the neck.
- Must not become a long scarf, cape, armor collar, bandana, or continuation of the shirt.
- Subtle tonal separation from both the mask and hoodie must remain visible.

### 4.6 Hood — LOCKED

- Raised hood is part of the default canonical silhouette.
- Broad charcoal outer hood forms a distinct pointed arch above the head.
- Dark inner lining frames the hair, temples, ears, and face without obscuring the waves completely.
- Hood opening is balanced and symmetrical in the frontal reference.
- Preserve the crown height, border thickness, and face-framing shape.
- Do not add ears, spikes, metallic trim, tech panels, glowing elements, or oversized fantasy proportions.
- A hood-down appearance requires explicit approval; it is not an automatic pose variation.

### 4.7 Hoodie/jacket — LOCKED

- Dark charcoal zip-front technical hoodie worn open.
- Clean, minimal construction with long sleeves, simple cuffs, lower front pockets, straight hem, and two dark drawstrings with small end caps.
- Zipper remains visually simple and unobtrusive.
- Fit is relaxed but not oversized; shoulders remain moderately broad and the torso stays lean.
- No logos, patches, slogans, armor plates, excessive pockets, or bright piping.

### 4.8 Corporate shirt — LOCKED

- Pale cool blue-gray button-down shirt with sparse vertical pinstripes.
- Shirt remains visible beneath the open hoodie and extends below the hoodie hem.
- Collar is present but partly obscured by the neck gaiter and lanyard.
- Center button placket and several small dark buttons remain visible when not occluded.
- Untucked split shirt tails form a clean corporate-meets-streetwear layer.
- Do not replace with a T-shirt, sweatshirt, tie, suit jacket, solid white dress shirt, or heavily patterned shirt.

### 4.9 Lanyard and badge treatment — LOCKED

- Plain muted navy lanyard worn in a clean V shape over the shirt and beneath the neck area.
- No lettering, company name, repeated microtext, or recognizable branding on the strap.
- Small simple connector/clip at the bottom of the V.
- Blank rectangular badge with a light face and dark/navy border.
- Badge contains no legible text, portrait, barcode, icon, or logo unless a later production shot supplies a separate composited design.
- Lanyard and badge are part of the canonical corporate identity and should normally remain present.

### 4.10 Lanyard behavior — FLEXIBLE

- The strap and badge may swing, tilt, bend, or become partly occluded by arms or props according to gravity and pose.
- The V-shaped origin, muted navy color, simple clip, and blank badge treatment remain unchanged.

### 4.11 Cargo pants — LOCKED

- Dark charcoal/near-black cargo pants with a clean tapered silhouette.
- One large, simple cargo pocket on the outside of each thigh; balanced bilateral construction.
- Minimal seam language and a small number of broad folds.
- Legs taper into dark ankle cuffs.
- No hanging straps, random mini-pouches, chains, knee armor, tactical webbing, camouflage, or asymmetrical pocket proliferation.

### 4.12 Footwear — LOCKED

- Generic chunky technical sneakers with the same overall profile and color blocking as the canonical image.
- Palette: light gray/soft white upper panels, medium-gray overlays, charcoal collar and outsole accents.
- Rounded athletic toe, layered but simplified panel construction, conventional laces, and substantial sculpted sole.
- No recognizable brand marks, high-top conversion, boots, dress shoes, glowing soles, or saturated accent colors.
- Exact lace crossings and tiny panel seams may simplify with scale, but the shoe category, silhouette, and grayscale color blocking remain stable.

## 5. Body, proportions, and silhouette

### 5.1 Proportions — LOCKED

- Adult, lean-to-athletic build; neither thin/frail nor muscular/bulky.
- Approximately 7.25–7.75 heads tall in neutral full-body views.
- Moderately broad shoulders, narrow-to-average waist, long legs, and balanced limb lengths.
- Hands are realistic and proportionate, large enough to hold props naturally but not exaggerated.
- Feet and sneakers are slightly substantial without becoming cartoonishly oversized.
- Maintain adult anatomy. No chibi, teenage, childlike, bodybuilder, superhero, or fashion-model exaggeration.

### 5.2 Silhouette — LOCKED

At small mobile size, the character must remain recognizable from:

- The pointed raised hood surrounding a low wave haircut.
- Strong masked eye band and layered neck gaiter.
- Moderately broad hoodie shoulders over a lean torso.
- Visible pale pinstripe shirt and navy lanyard breaking up the dark clothing mass.
- Tapered cargo-pant legs with one large pocket per outer thigh.
- Chunky light-gray technical sneakers.

The silhouette should stay compact enough to occupy the lower-left or side of a 1080×1920 composition without blocking the primary diagram or interface area.

### 5.3 Pose and anatomy — FLEXIBLE

- Standing, three-quarter, seated, walking, pointing, presenting, thinking, typing, or device-holding poses are allowed.
- Arm position, hand gestures, stance width, weight distribution, torso rotation, and head angle may change naturally.
- Perspective may foreshorten limbs, but underlying proportions must remain consistent.
- Hands must be anatomically clean and interact believably with props.

## 7. Rendering and line style

### 7.1 Rendering style — LOCKED

- Clean contemporary anime-inspired character illustration.
- Realistic adult proportions rather than stylized manga exaggeration.
- Crisp dark outer contour and controlled thinner interior linework.
- Restrained two- to three-value cel shading with limited soft gradients.
- Clear shape design and mobile-scale readability take priority over texture detail.
- Moderate material differentiation: matte fabric, smooth badge, and slightly reflective device surfaces.
- Polished production illustration, not sketch art, painterly concept art, 3D render, comic halftone, photorealism, pixel art, or vector-flat corporate illustration.

### 7.2 Lighting — FLEXIBLE

- Lighting direction, intensity, rim light, and shadow placement may adapt to the scene.
- Facial identity, skin tone, palette hierarchy, and readable separation among mask, gaiter, hood, and hoodie must survive the lighting change.
- Avoid extreme colored lighting unless explicitly required by the scene.

### 7.3 Detail density — FLEXIBLE within limits

- Full-body mobile assets should use fewer interior lines than close-ups.
- Close-ups may reveal additional wave texture, fabric folds, eye detail, and shoe construction, but may not invent new design features.
- Tiny stitching, lace intersections, and stripe count may simplify at small scale.

## 8. Optional elements and props

### 8.1 Tablet — OPTIONAL/PROP

- The tablet is not part of the character's anatomy or permanent wardrobe.
- When present, it is held in landscape orientation unless the shot requires otherwise.
- Sturdy dark frame with a thin pale cyan edge accent.
- Blank, bounded, slightly opaque cool blue-gray screen.
- No baked-in interface, logo, text, icon, diagram, or brand mark.
- Screen content should be composited separately whenever possible.
- Do not show the shirt or badge through the screen as if the tablet were transparent glass.

### 8.2 Other props — OPTIONAL/PROP

- Phone, stylus, pointer, laptop, clipboard, package, or other script-relevant objects may be introduced per shot.
- Props must not permanently alter the character design.
- Brand logos, interface content, and readable text should generally remain separate compositing assets.
- Weapons, fantasy artifacts, tactical gear, and decorative cyberpunk hardware are outside the established character concept unless explicitly requested.

### 8.3 Ground shadow — OPTIONAL/PROP

- Default production cutouts should not contain a baked-in ground shadow.
- If needed, the shadow should be delivered as a separate layer or generated by the motion-graphics system.

## 9. Production asset rules

### 9.1 Default delivery

- Transparent PNG with genuine alpha.
- Clean anti-aliased edges without white, gray, or black background halos.
- No background, floor, vignette, environmental lighting effect, text, logo, diagram, dashboard, or interface unless explicitly requested.
- Do not crop the hood, hands, elbows, clothing hem, or feet when a full-body asset is requested.
- Maintain safe transparent padding around the silhouette.

### 9.2 Motion-graphics compatibility

- The character commonly occupies the lower-left or side of a vertical 1080×1920 frame.
- Gestures and gaze should direct attention toward the open information area.
- Avoid unnecessarily wide props or arm poses that consume the diagram/UI zone.
- Keep the silhouette readable against both light and dark backgrounds.
- Tablet screen, foreground hands, badge, and other overlapping parts should be separated into layers when animation or screen replacement is planned.

### 9.3 Recommended layered structure when needed

1. Character base/body.
2. Foreground arm and hand occlusion.
3. Lanyard/badge if independent motion is required.
4. Prop frame.
5. Replaceable prop screen/content area.
6. Optional reflection/highlight.
7. Optional separate ground shadow.

## 10. Explicit anti-drift rules

Reject or revise any output that introduces:

- Braids, twists, dreadlocks, cornrows, hanging curls, or a skin-close bald/buzzed appearance.
- Different ethnicity, lighter skin, or significantly different facial structure.
- Round, oversized, glowing, or youthful anime eyes.
- Missing mask, merged mask/gaiter, exposed mouth, or printed mask graphics.
- Hood-down styling, radically different hood geometry, or added hood ornaments.
- Closed hoodie, missing pinstripe shirt, T-shirt replacement, tie, suit, or armor.
- Branded or text-covered lanyard/badge.
- Asymmetrical tactical pants, hanging straps, excess pouches, chains, or knee armor.
- Branded shoes, boots, or saturated shoe accents.
- Superhero musculature, chibi proportions, child/teen proportions, or exaggerated fashion anatomy.
- Photorealism, 3D rendering, painterly concept art, sketchiness, or neon cyberpunk redesign.
- Baked-in diagrams, UI, text, logos, or scene backgrounds on reusable character assets.

## 12. Pose-generation prompt structure

Every production request should contain:

1. The canonical image as the identity reference.
2. The fixed reproduction identity block above.
3. The requested pose and camera angle.
4. The intended on-screen placement and gaze/gesture direction.
5. Required props and which parts need separate layers.
6. Transparent-background and no-baked-content requirements.
7. The anti-drift rules relevant to that shot.

Generate and approve a small number of priority poses before expanding the library. Do not create a large batch from text alone.

## 13. Production QA checklist

An asset passes only when all applicable checks are true:

- [ ] Same adult Black male facial identity as the canonical image.
- [ ] Short visible 360 waves and clean hairline; no braids or alternate hairstyle.
- [ ] Eye shape, brow geometry, skin tone, and forehead proportions match.
- [ ] Fitted mask and separate folded neck gaiter are both present.
- [ ] Raised pointed hood retains the canonical framing and silhouette.
- [ ] Open charcoal hoodie, pale pinstripe corporate shirt, navy lanyard, and blank badge remain correct.
- [ ] Lean adult proportions and long-legged silhouette match.
- [ ] Cargo-pant structure and grayscale technical shoes remain consistent.
- [ ] Anime linework, restrained cel shading, and detail density match.
- [ ] Pose is anatomically sound; hands and prop interactions are clean.
- [ ] No unauthorized redesign, branding, text, UI, or extra accessories.
- [ ] Transparent edges are clean and the asset has adequate padding.
- [ ] Optional props are removable or separately compositable where required.
