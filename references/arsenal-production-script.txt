Mara runs a small roofing crew, and the work starts before anyone climbs a ladder.
A lead is not a feeling. It is a message that has to land somewhere you can find it again.
Open the public page, the one at example.com, and look at the search the way a buyer would.
That page is not the business. It is the evidence that someone is already asking for the work.
From there the path is a simple mechanism. The inquiry comes in, the form records the address, and the sheet lists the calls.
One hundred calls is not one hundred jobs. Most of them are noise, and the picture should show the count before it shows a winner.
The inbox is where the real ones sit. A reply, a missed call, a quote request. Each one changes the record.
The form is the gate. If the address is outside the crew's area, the lead stops. If it is inside, the lead moves.
A diagram of that gate is more honest than another icon. Inquiry, check, quote, or refuse.
The quote is a document. It states the price, the scope, and the date. It does not become a job until the buyer accepts it.
Then the calendar holds the day, and the crew can see the work as a sequence instead of a pile.
If the buyer goes quiet, the lead stays a lead. The quote does not pretend the job is booked.
The recap is the same lead, still named, with the states it actually earned. Message, check, quote, day.
