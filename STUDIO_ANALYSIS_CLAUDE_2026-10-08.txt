1. EXECUTIVE DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

These two workflows are a DIFFERENT PRODUCT than ours.

Both are assembly-line editing of existing footage: record a human talking,
transcribe it, cut mistakes, overlay titles and B-roll, score it, export.
Our system generates a designed film from a script. There is no footage, no
cuts to find, no dead air to remove. Roughly 60% of what these transcripts
contain has no referent in our pipeline.

They are worth reading because they are strong on exactly the three things
our architecture is weakest on, and all three are INFRASTRUCTURE, NOT
CREATIVITY:

  (1) THE FINISHER IS A NAMED JOB.
      FFmpeg checks black screens and sync before the human ever sees the
      video. Our server/render.ts (lines 26-42) renders and reports
      progress. There is no post-render validation of any kind -- not
      codec, not duration, not audio presence, not black frames. The Mara
      film reached independent review with 20 state-leak defects in it and
      nothing in the pipeline could have objected.

  (2) FEEDBACK HAS A DESTINATION.
      "When you give it feedback, tell it to save what it's learned to the
      skill itself." Our studio memory is far better DESIGNED than a
      1,400-line append-only file, but it has no defined producer, no
      provenance on most entries, and no expiry.

      Proof inside our own repo: MASTER_HANDOFF section 0 promotes composed
      stages and the bespoke React/SVG path into production; section 12
      (lines 617-620) still asserts "no multi-component constructed stage
      yet / no bespoke React/SVG scene path yet". The file resolves this
      with a prose disclaimer at line 128. An agent that reads section 12
      without section 0 acts on a false constraint.

      OUR SOURCE OF TRUTH IS CURRENTLY SELF-CONTRADICTING, AND THE ONLY
      GUARD IS THAT A HUMAN REMEMBERS TO READ THE HEADER.

  (3) THE AGENT VERIFIES ITS OWN OUTPUT BEFORE HANDING IT OVER.
      Transcript 2 calls this "probably the most important one."

Point (3) cuts in our favour. The currently approved RENDER TRUTH & VISUAL
ACTION GATE is already the correct response to the strongest idea in both
transcripts. inspect-render.ts and gap-regression.ts are precisely that
verification loop. The transcripts VALIDATE the active phase rather than
redirecting it. No architectural blocker was found. Nothing here supersedes
the gate.

CORRECTION TO A COMMON READING: the seven "roles" in transcript 1 are seven
TOOLS, not seven agents. One model coordinates all of them. The transcript
does not argue for multi-agent orchestration, and nothing in it is evidence
that we need more agents.

3. THE GENUINELY MISSING PIECES
================================================================================

Three. None requires a new framework.


3.1 DETERMINISTIC POST-RENDER QC                                           [C]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing inspects the MP4. Split into three tiers and keep them separate.

  TIER 1 — CONTAINER / TECHNICAL (ffmpeg, deterministic)
    - resolution, fps
    - duration within +/- 0.5s of plan.durationSec
    - audio stream present and AAC
    - audio duration approximately equals video duration
    - no silent regions where narration is scheduled
    - blackdetect for blank frames
    - freezedetect for a stage that renders nothing
    - SAFE-ZONE assertion: no presenter or caption pixel inside the
      TikTok / Reels control rectangles.
      (Independent review flagged lower-left platform safety as
       undemonstrated -- MASTER_HANDOFF line 34. This is mechanical,
       not aesthetic.)
    ffmpeg is already a de facto dependency: inspect-render.ts line 48
    shells out to it.

  TIER 2 — PLAN vs RENDER CONFORMANCE (deterministic, cheap, absent,
           HIGHEST VALUE)
    We have word timestamps. For sampled frames, assert:
    - the caption text matches the word expected at that timestamp
    - the spine's active index matches the chapter
    - THE STATUS CHIP EQUALS beat.state

    That last check alone would have caught all 20 inter-beat leaks
    directly from the MP4, with no vision model and no plan simulation.
    It generalizes to every future script for free.
    This is the single cheapest high-value item in the entire analysis.

  TIER 3 — ART DIRECTION (vision, judgment)
    inspect-render.ts. Keep strictly separate. Never let it arbitrate
    anything tiers 1-2 can prove.


3.2 GOVERNED FEEDBACK -> STUDIO MEMORY                                     [C]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We have excellent memory ARTIFACTS and no memory PROCESS.

  - No defined producer. Docs are edited by whichever agent happens to be
    writing a handoff.
  - No provenance outside G01-G13. The section-0 reconciliations carry no
    evidence IDs.
  - No expiry or review trigger -- exactly why MASTER_HANDOFF section 12
    went stale while remaining authoritative.
  - src/film/style-guide.ts -- the ONLY memory the model actually receives
    at direction time -- is a hand-maintained 12-line excerpt of a 217-line
    guide. Nothing keeps them in sync. If a G-rule changes, styleRules
    silently does not.

The four founder observations (presenter too mechanically anchored, static
cards feel wrong, transitions feel like clips under clips, visuals should
behave like the references) DID reach the documents -- but via an expensive
Opus analysis plus an independent audiovisual review. That is a heroic
process, not a repeatable one.

DO NOT COPY THEIR APPEND-TO-SKILL REFLEX. Their skill grows forever and a
one-off preference looks identical to a load-bearing rule. The correct shape
is a PROMOTION LADDER (section 6 below).

KEY ASYMMETRY: the strongest form of studio memory is not a longer document.
It is a SHORTER document plus a TEST. Our own gate already demonstrates
this: "hold the last started beat during silence" did not become a style
rule. It became hold.ts plus gap-regression.ts. That is the model.

HARD RULE TO ADOPT: A BUG NEVER BECOMES A STYLE RULE.
prod-builder/NOTES.md line 13 records that the premature Tools steps were a
RENDERER TIMING BUG. Appended to a skill as "don't show the replacement
early", that would have become a permanent prose rule masking a code defect.


3.3 AN EXECUTABLE ACTION VOCABULARY                                        [C]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our own documents have independently converged on this.
MOTION_STYLE_GUIDE section 8: "G01/G02/G03/G07 improvement must be
demonstrated by rendered action rather than additional prompt language."

THE PRECISE GAP: the reference work produced RULES, not VERBS.

The system has NOUNS -- 135 components, 7 graphics, one BespokeDiagram
schema -- and no VERBS. Yet P1-P6 in our own style guide are entirely
verbs: request marks APPROACH a counter; a brain CONNECTS THROUGH hands to
windows; a row CHANGES from Draft to Ready; the view PULLS BACK.

tools-action.tsx proves the substrate is fine -- 92 lines of plain SVG
travels a token across an API boundary using clock.progress. But it is
reached by StructuredFilm.tsx line 93:

    chapter.name === "Tools" ? <ToolsAction /> : ...

A hardcoded string match on the chapter name, bypassing FilmPiece entirely.
Legitimate as a gate prototype. It cannot survive. Any future film with a
chapter named "Tools" renders the Mara API diagram.

GENERALIZATION (after the gate passes): a small parameterized verb set --
traverse, accumulate, select, block/refuse, transform, return -- in the
bespoke path, chosen by stageSystem by SHAPE OF ACTION exactly as graphics
are chosen by SHAPE OF INFORMATION.

That requires a BESPOKE / ACTION REGISTRY mirroring registry.tsx, so new
construction methods become DATA rather than renderer edits. That registry
is the one piece of infrastructure worth establishing to keep future tool
pluralism from making the renderer fragile.

4. WHAT WE ALREADY DO BETTER — DO NOT REINVENT
================================================================================

  TIMING AUTHORITY
    They transcribe recorded speech with ASR to guess where graphics go. We
    synthesize from a known script and receive word timestamps from the
    generator: 100% token match, no inference error, and the alignment bug
    class is already fixed and regression-covered. Categorically stronger,
    and it is the foundation everything else rests on.

  STUDIO MEMORY DESIGN
    Authority ordering, LOCKED / FLEXIBLE / OPTIONAL status labels,
    evidence IDs, explicit scope limits ("this guide cannot silently amend
    them"), and a stated refusal to promote unmeasured timing/easing
    constants. Their skill has none of this. Our weakness is PROCESS, not
    DESIGN -- do not let the fix degrade the design.

  COVERAGE AS A HARD FAILURE
    directStructure throws when a sentence has no chapter. These workflows
    would silently drop content and nobody would know.

  PLACEMENT AUTHORITY
    layoutFor as the only placer, after a documented raw-coordinate
    failure. Neither transcript has an equivalent; both are one bad prompt
    away from the failure mode we already escaped.

  REPAIR GRANULARITY
    Targeted re-beat / re-stage of up to four weak chapters with the rest
    preserved. Their loop is "re-prompt the whole thing."

  IDENTITY GOVERNANCE
    The Character Bible's per-attribute lock discipline is far beyond
    "character bible" as either transcript uses the term.

  HONEST VALIDATION
    The system produced a technically perfect render -- correct resolution,
    100% alignment, truthful data model -- and our process REFUSED TO
    APPROVE IT. That is a stronger quality culture than anything in these
    videos, where the standard is "it's not perfect, right? But look how
    good this was in one prompt."

6. RECOMMENDED FUTURE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Nothing below changes the four-layer grammar, layoutFor, the narration
clock, or the structured passes.

    script (supplied, verbatim)
      -> structureSystem            whole film
      -> beatSystem  x N
      -> stageSystem x N  --------+
      -> inspectPlan (deterministic, plan-level)        [EXISTS]
      -> targeted repair                                [EXISTS]
      -> render                                         [EXISTS]
         |
         +-- TIER 1  technical QC
         |           ffmpeg: codec, duration, audio, black, freeze,
         |           safe-zone                                    <- NEW [C]
         +-- TIER 2  plan vs render conformance
         |           caption / spine / chip vs expected at sampled
         |           frames                                       <- NEW [C]
         +-- TIER 3  visual critique
                     inspect-render.ts, vision, judgment      [IN FLIGHT]
         |
      -> targeted repair -> re-render
      -> deliverable

            +------------ stage construction backends ------------+
            |  library | procedural | bespoke diagram | ACTION VERBS <- NEW [C]
            |                     via a BESPOKE / ACTION REGISTRY   <- NEW [B]
            |            (replaces  chapter.name === "Tools")
            +-----------------------------------------------------+


    STUDIO LEARNING LOOP  (separate, slower, explicitly NOT per-film)
                                                                  <- NEW [C]
      N films + founder feedback
        -> OBSERVATION   film NOTES.md, timestamped -- never a rule
        -> CANDIDATE     >= 2 distinct films OR a named code root cause;
                         staged, labeled
        -> PROMOTION     choose the strongest available form:
                           (a) CODE CONSTRAINT  if deterministically
                               checkable            <- PREFERRED
                           (b) G-RULE  evidence IDs + reject clause +
                               scope + review trigger
                           (c) CAPABILITY  a renderer channel, not a
                               sentence
        -> styleRules.ts DERIVED from the guide, never hand-maintained
        -> periodic PRUNE  (memory must shrink as well as grow)


TWO LOOPS, EXPLICITLY DISTINGUISHED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PER-FILM LOOP
    plan -> render -> QC + critic -> targeted repair -> re-render
    Scope: one film. Artifact: a repaired MP4 and its NOTES.md.
    NEVER TOUCHES STUDIO MEMORY.

  STUDIO LOOP
    Multiple films + founder feedback -> promotion ladder above.
    Artifact: a governed diff with provenance.


EVIDENCE THRESHOLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  OBSERVATION       One film, one moment, founder note.
                    Lives in that film's NOTES.md, timestamped.

  CANDIDATE         Reproduced in >= 2 DISTINCT FILMS (not 2 chapters of
                    one film), or traced to a named code root cause.
                    Lives in a staging section, explicitly labeled.

  APPROVED RULE     Candidate + one rendered counter-example + a reject
                    clause + evidence IDs + a REVIEW TRIGGER.
                    Lives in MOTION_STYLE_GUIDE G-rules.

  CODE CONSTRAINT   Any rule that is deterministically checkable.
                    Lives in code + a regression test.
                    ALWAYS PREFERRED.

  NEVER PROMOTE     Anything whose root cause is a code defect.
                    Anything observed in a single frame.
                    Any timing / easing / density / occupancy number
                    without measurement (the guide already forbids this --
                    keep it).

  The missing field everywhere today is the REVIEW TRIGGER -- e.g.
  "re-examine when the renderer gains action primitives". Its absence is
  precisely why section 12 went stale while remaining authoritative.

NOTE ON THE RESEARCHER ROLE AND PROVENANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a script ever DOES make a verifiable real-world claim, the right design
is not "fetch B-roll" -- it is a CLAIM GATE. The structure pass flags the
factual claim; it must either be sourced and displayed with provenance, or
removed from the script. That matches our existing policy (fictional cases
allowed, fabricated real-world facts forbidden) rather than bolting on a
research agent.

Today this is a solution without a problem: no script to date cites an
external fact.

HEADLINE DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================
The hypothesis (a missing film-level creative-direction layer) was CORRECT IN
ITS CONCLUSION AND WRONG IN ITS CAUSAL ORDERING.

The precise statement of the hole:

    NOTHING IN THE SYSTEM IS BOTH FILM-SPECIFIC AND VISUAL.

  - creative-standard.md, MOTION_STYLE_GUIDE.md, the Character Bible,
    styleRules: visual, but FILM-INVARIANT.
  - the catalog and stage-fit.json: visual, but CONTENT-INVARIANT.
  - the script: film-specific, but VERBAL.
  - structure.json: film-specific, but ORGANIZATIONAL, NOT VISUAL.

Of ChapterBrief's eleven fields, only title, spine and example are
film-level, and each is a LABEL. structureSystem is a DECOMPOSITION pass
that reads the whole script, not an INTERPRETATION pass that conceives the
whole film.

The only information that travels between chapters (direct-structure.ts
lines 244-246):

    const avoid = previous ? [previous.component || previous.graphic] : [];

A de-duplication blacklist. NEGATIVE COORDINATION STANDING IN FOR DIRECTION.
The system knows "don't use record again". It cannot know "chapter 5 shows
the inbox from chapter 1, now resolved."

WHY A DIRECTOR'S BRIEF IS NOT THE SAME AS A BIGGER PROMPT
================================================================================
styleRules is ALREADY the failure mode: twelve lines of excellent creative
advice injected identically into all three passes, including G01 and G06
verbatim -- and C1/C2/C3 in the motion guide are precisely G01/G06
violations in the render.

A BIGGER PROMPT CHANGES WHAT A PASS IS TOLD TO VALUE.
A CREATIVE-DIRECTION LAYER CHANGES WHAT A PASS KNOWS.

Three tests. Real direction:
  1. produces a durable artifact alongside structure.json and plan.json,
     diffable, reviewable before any render;
  2. causes a later chapter's decision to change because of a DIFFERENT
     chapter's decision -- not via a blacklist;
  3. can be wrong in a way that is detectable and repairable pre-render.

At the time of writing: zero of three.
